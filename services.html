Pricing logic

Distance is only one part.

A professional quote should explain what changes the price.

01 — Distance

Origin-to-destination kilometres influence fuel, vehicle cost and transit time.

02 — Volume

More cartons, furniture and fragile items require more packing, labour and vehicle space.

03 — Access

Floor, lift, narrow lanes, waiting time and special handling can change the estimate.
