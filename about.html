Our process

Simple from enquiry to delivery.

1. Share details

Pickup, destination, distance, inventory and preferred date.

2. Get estimate

Route, volume, access and service requirements are checked.

3. Pack & load

Items are packed, labelled and loaded according to the agreed scope.

4. Transit

Consignment moves according to the planned route and schedule.

5. Delivery

Goods are unloaded and handed over at the destination.

6. Feedback

Customer feedback can be collected after successful delivery.

FAQ

Common customer questions.

Does the online price include packing?

It depends on the selected service. The real site should clearly separate packing, labour, transport, tolls and optional insurance.

Can you transport cars and bikes?

Yes, this demo includes both categories. The real business should list only routes and vehicle types it actually serves.

How is the final price calculated?

Distance, shipment volume, vehicle type, packing scope, floor/lift access, route and special handling can affect the final amount.
